A look inside

What it does

  • 30 gods, six pantheons. Greek, Norse, Egyptian, Japanese, Aztec and Celtic, five gods each, all with their own painted card art.
  • Classic trump card rules. Pick a stat from your top card. Higher wins both cards. Ties go to the middle for the next round.
  • Win the whole deck. The game ends when one player owns all 30 cards.
  • A computer opponent that plays its own top card using what it knows about the deck.
  • Watch AI vs AI. Sit back and watch two computer players battle it out.
  • Deck browser. Flip through every card and its stats.
  • Mouse, keyboard or controller. Play however you like.
