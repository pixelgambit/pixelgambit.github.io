Fastline privacy policy

Last updated November 2026.

Fastline is a fasting tracker for Android, published by pixelgambit.

What Fastline stores

Your fasts, your weight entries, your notes and your settings. All of it is written to storage on this phone and nowhere else.

What Fastline sends

Nothing. There is no account to make, no server to sign in to, and no analytics, advertising or tracking code in the app. Fastline makes no network connections of its own, so your data cannot leave this phone unless you move it yourself.

Backups you make

Back up my data writes your information to a single file and hands it to Android, which asks where you want it to go. If you choose Drive, email or another app, that file is then covered by whatever that service does with your files. Fastline has no part in it after the handover, and makes no backup you did not ask for.

Tips

Tip with PayPal in Settings opens a PayPal page in your browser. Fastline sends nothing with it, and never learns whether you tipped. Anything you do on that page is between you and PayPal, under their privacy policy.

Notifications

Reminders and stage alerts are scheduled on the phone by Android itself. They are not push messages, nothing is sent over the internet to deliver them, and you can switch any of them off in Settings.

Permissions

Fastline asks to show notifications, which you can decline and still use everything else. Backing up or restoring opens the system file picker, which gives the app only the one file you choose.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Fastline removes everything it has stored. Any backup file you made yourself stays wherever you put it, so delete that separately if you want it gone.

Children

Fastline is not intended for anyone under 18 and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date on it changes with it. Questions about this policy or your data can go to ericdekock@gmail.com.

Not medical advice

Fastline is a wellness tracker, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition, and nothing in it is medical advice. Talk to a doctor before you change how you eat, and never delay or ignore their advice because of something you read in this app.

Fastline is made for adults. It is not for anyone under 18, who needs regular meals to grow properly.

Who should talk to a doctor first

Fasting is not right for everyone. Please talk to a doctor before you start if any of these apply to you:

  • You are pregnant or breastfeeding
  • You have a history of disordered eating, or you are underweight
  • You have diabetes, or you use insulin
  • You take diuretics, blood pressure medication, or anything else sensitive to blood sugar or electrolytes
  • You are recovering from major surgery
  • You have a liver, kidney or heart condition
  • You drink heavily, or you are recovering from alcohol dependence

About the fasting stages

The fasting stages come from published research on what typically happens over a fast. They are not readings from your body. Fastline cannot measure your glucose, ketones or hormones, and real timings shift with your last meal, your sleep, your activity and plenty else. Treat every stage as a rough guide, not a result.
