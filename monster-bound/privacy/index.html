Monster Bound privacy policy

Last updated November 2026.

Monster Bound is a card game for Android, published by pixelgambit.

What Monster Bound stores

Your run in progress, your settings, your records and the reports of your recent runs. All of it is written to storage on this phone and nowhere else.

What Monster Bound sends

Nothing. There is no account to make, no server to sign in to, and no analytics, advertising or tracking code in the game. Monster Bound does not ask for internet access, so your data cannot leave this phone.

Tips

Tip with PayPal in Settings asks first, then opens a PayPal page in your browser. Monster Bound sends nothing with it, and never learns whether you tipped. A tip unlocks nothing in the game. Anything you do on that page is between you and PayPal, under their privacy policy.

Permissions

Monster Bound asks for one permission: to vibrate, so you can feel a blow land. You can switch vibration off in Settings.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Monster Bound removes everything it has stored. Clearing the app's storage in Android's settings does the same without uninstalling it.

Children

Monster Bound collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date on it changes with it. Questions about this policy can go to ericdekock@gmail.com.
