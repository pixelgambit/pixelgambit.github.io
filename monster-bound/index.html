Monster Bound

AndroidClosed test open

A minimalist roguelike card crawler. Fight your way through a dungeon built from a single deck of cards, using weapons, potions and careful risk-taking to survive one room at a time.

Become a tester

A look inside

What it does

  • A 44-card dungeon. Every run is one shuffled deck of monsters, weapons and potions. Clear it and you win.
  • Four cards per room. Resolve three, leave one behind, or escape the room and face it later.
  • Weapons that wear down. Once a weapon has slain a monster, it can only be used on weaker ones after that.
  • Potion timing matters. Only one potion heals per room, so when you drink is half the game.
  • Run stats and a real score. See monsters slain, damage blocked, your longest weapon streak and your final score.
  • Fully offline. No account, no ads, no network. Just you and the deck.

How to join the test

Monster Bound is in a closed test on Google Play. You'll need an Android phone and the Google account you use for Play.

  1. Join the testers group

    Join the Pixel Gambit testers Google Group with the same Google account you use on your phone.

    Join the group
  2. Opt in on Google Play

    Open the testing page and tap "Become a tester". It can take a few minutes after joining the group before this works.

    Open the testing page
  3. Install Monster Bound from Play

    Download it from the Play Store link on the testing page and play a few runs.

  4. Keep it installed for 14 days

    Google needs testers to stay opted in for 14 days before the game can launch.

Monster Bound is inspired by Scoundrel, the solo card game by Zach Gage and Kurt Bieg.
