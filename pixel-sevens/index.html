Pixel Sevens

PCControllerTesters wanted

Top-down, 8-bit style rugby sevens. Fast matches, real laws, 16 national teams and a full tournament mode, made to be played with a controller.

Become a tester

What it does

  • Real sevens rules. Tackles, rucks, penalties, conversions and golden-point extra time, played the way sevens is.
  • 16 national teams, each with its own kit, ratings and style of play.
  • Tournaments. Take a team through a full sevens tournament.
  • 2-player. Play a friend on the same screen.
  • Kicking minigames. Practise conversions and penalty kicks.
  • A computer opponent tuned against real sevens match statistics.
  • Watch AI vs AI. Let the computer play both sides and see how a match unfolds.
